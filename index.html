What can you do in ?

1. Make a Mii to Look Like You
 
Miitomo features the Mii characters that debuted on Nintendo's Wii system. Choose from a wide library of facial features to make your Mii look just like you. You can also create one by using a photo taken by your smart device's camera.
After creating your Mii and giving it a nickname, you can customize how it speaks and choose its personality.
 
2. Use Your Mii as a Social Go-Between
 
In Miitomo, your Mii and your friends' Mii characters act as social go-betweens. Your Mii will ask you questions you'd never think to ask yourself, to learn the fun little things that make you...YOU!
Your friends' Mii characters will do the same thing. Then your Mii and their Mii characters tell each other about these details, so you and your friends learn fun things about each other that you'd never find out without Miitomo!
And using special Sidekick Mii characters, you can send messages directly to your friends!
 
 
3. Dress Your Mii However You Want
 
Costumes, streetwear, formal fashion, everyday style—you can find it all in the Miitomo shop. Mix and match clothing items to create just the perfect look for your Mii to wear!
You can get clothing items with Miitomo coins, which you earn by playing Miitomo.
You can also purchase Miitomo coins.
 
4. Customize Your Room to Perfection
 
Unleash your inner interior designer with wallpaper and flooring styles up for grabs through the Miitomo Drop game.
You can even make in-app posters out of images saved on your device!
 
5. Win Prizes From Miitomo Drop
 
Miitomo features a minigame in which you drop a Mii into a play field, and then watch it bounce around as it falls toward a variety of platforms.
You'll win a prize based on where it lands. Some clothing items are available only in Miitomo Drop.
To play Miitomo Drop, you need Miitomo coins, which you earn by playing Miitomo or by using game tickets, which you can get as a daily bonus, among other ways.
You can also purchase Miitomo coins.
 
6. Make Your Mii a Star with Miifotos
 
In Miitomo, you can create images called Miifotos. You can let your creativity go wild, putting your Mii and friends' Mii characters in images you make from a library of stamps, fonts, and backgrounds.
You can also use photos from a smart device's camera as backgrounds for Miifoto creations.
You can share your Miifoto creations on social media, such as Twitter, Facebook, and Instagram.
